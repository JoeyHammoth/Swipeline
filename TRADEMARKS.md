# Trademarks

The Apache License 2.0 grants copyright and patent rights to the code, but it
does not grant trademark rights. This page describes how the Swipeline name and
logo may be used.

## You may

- Use the name **Swipeline** to refer to this project, its packages and its
  documentation, including in articles, talks, tutorials and comparisons.
- State that your product is "built with Swipeline", "uses Swipeline" or
  "is compatible with Swipeline", provided it is true.
- Use the logo to link to this repository or the documentation site.
- Use the `@swipeline` npm scope only for packages published by this project.
  Community adapters use the `swipeline-adapter-<name>` naming convention.

## You may not

- Use the Swipeline name or logo as the name or branding of your own product,
  company, app or hosted service.
- Imply endorsement, sponsorship or official status that does not exist.
- Register a domain, social handle, npm package or organisation that could be
  confused with this project.

## Community adapters and bindings

Community packages may say "for Swipeline" or "Swipeline adapter for X" in their
description, but their package name must not begin with `@swipeline/`. They must
not use the project logo as their own icon.

## Reporting misuse

Open an issue or contact a maintainer. Genuine confusion is usually resolved
with a rename request, not a legal process.

Questions about this policy: open a discussion in the repository.
